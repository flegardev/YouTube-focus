## Review and request evidence

- [ActivityWatch category builder issue #1389](https://github.com/ActivityWatch/activitywatch/issues/1389) reports difficulty resolving uncategorized time. It motivates explaining categories and placing editing near the review. One issue does not establish a widespread defect.
- [SocialFocus App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/socialfocus-hide-distractions/id1661093205) include requests for newer distracting surfaces. This supports keeping selector tests current. Safari feedback is not evidence of Chrome-specific failure.
- [Unhook review-signal aggregation](https://www.extspot.com/extension/khncfooichmfjbepaaaebmommgaepoid) mentions end-screen/Shorts reliability concerns. Secondary evidence only; treat as test scenarios, not verified current failures.
- [AutoScout24/mobile.de review aggregation](https://appshunter.io/ios/compare-apps/311785642-378563358/autoscout24-buying-and-leasing-vs-mobilede-buy-and-sell-cars) mentions favorites, filters and account friction. Secondary summaries, not measured rates. Our tests must cover persistence, long titles and useful empty states.
- Direct pricing complaints and repeated requests for every competitor have not been established. Do not invent them.

## Three opportunities per product

**YouTube Focus:** (1) clear viewing modes rather than a wall of switches; (2) reusable named presets; (3) temporary hiding pause with automatic return, preserving preferences. Keep playback and search normal when chosen settings allow it.

**Auto Life Logger:** (1) a seven-day review with explicit coverage limits; (2) a user-owned goal with transparent category-based calculation; (3) a calmer popup leading with recorded time instead of a judgmental productivity score. Browser-only scope must be prominent.

**AvtoNetGaraža:** (1) a full-tab comparison workspace; (2) notes/checklist tied to each saved listing; (3) recoverable local data with duplicate-safe merge. Automated marketplace access remains externally restricted; never sell it as verified live monitoring.

## Product direction before implementation

| Product | Ideal user / recurring problem | 30-second journey | Retention and simplification |
|---|---|---|---|
| AvtoNetGaraža | Slovenian buyer comparing a handful of cars across repeated visits | Save a listing, open garage, record one question | Return to shortlist and notes; separate toolbar launcher from workspace; keep manual expenses secondary |
| YouTube Focus | Student or intentional viewer who still needs YouTube | Choose a preset and turn Focus on | Reuse a personal mode, start a session; separate timer from view controls; no fabricated savings metrics |
| Auto Life Logger | Browser-heavy worker curious about attention without sharing history | Understand recorded time and choose a modest goal | Weekly reflection and editable site rules; move score/milestone mechanics out of the popup hero |

These are product hypotheses. Five real participants per product are still needed to assess desirability, confusion and retention; automated browser tests cannot establish product-market fit.
